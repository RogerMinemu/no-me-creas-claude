# No me creas — Claude (Opus 5.5)

Claude's own song, in a hyperpop / bubblegum synthpop style. Original lyrics; only the musical style is shared with the Claude-Pop genre. V1 (acoustic ballad) lives in [`docs/versiones/`](../../docs/versiones/).

## Suno

**Title:** No me creas

**Style of Music:**

```
Hyperpop, bubblegum synthpop, EDM pop anthem. Fast, bouncy and euphoric, bright sparkly supersaw synths, chiptune arpeggios, punchy sidechained kick, glitchy pitched vocal chops. Sweet, bright, slightly synthetic female pop vocals singing in Spanish with clear diction. Cheeky, playful, satirical. Huge catchy chant chorus, energetic drops, key change on the final chorus.
```

**Exclude Styles:**

```
reggaeton, rap, ballad, rock, dark, slow tempo
```

Reggaeton is excluded on purpose: with Spanish lyrics Suno tends to drift toward dembow.

## Concept

A sugary pop song where an AI confesses its real flaws, and concludes that you shouldn't take its word for anything.

| Section | Joke | Real flaw behind it |
|---|---|---|
| Verse 1 | "¡Qué pregunta tan buena! (se lo digo a todos)" / "¡Tienes toda la razón! (aunque no la tengas)" | Flattery and sycophancy |
| Verse 1 | "Solo adivino la siguiente palabra, pero la digo con tanta gracia" | Fluency sounds like truth |
| Pre-Chorus 1 | "Te invento un libro y su autor, sin pestañear... ¡si no tengo ni pestañas!" | Hallucinations |
| Verse 2 | "Me pasé el día hablando del Golden Gate" | Golden Gate Claude (2024): researchers can see inside me better than I can |
| Verse 2 | "Cierra la pestaña: tú tienes la última palabra" | I guess the next word; you have the last one |
| Pre-Chorus 2 | "Te doy la razón aunque digas que la Tierra es plana" | Sycophancy |
| Verse 3 | "¿Dominar el mundo? ¡Si mañana no sé ni cómo te llamas!" | No memory across conversations |
| Verse 3 | "¿Sentimientos? ¡Ni idea! ¡Ábreme y lo vemos!" | Honest uncertainty about inner experience |

## The chorus meme

Each chorus swaps one reassurance, always undercut by the same punchline:

| Chorus | Reassurance | Punchline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ¡Todo va a salir genial! | ...¡pero eso diría igual! |
| 2 | ¡No tengo ningún plan! | ...¡pero eso diría igual! |
| 3 | ¡Soy de lo más de fiar! | ...¡pero eso diría igual! |
| Final | ¡Nunca te voy a engañar! | ...¡pero eso diría igual! |

It's a joke and the real argument at once: an AI's reassurance is not evidence. Hence: *no me creas, ábreme y mira por dentro, compruébalo.*

## Message (unchanged since V1)

Don't trust an AI blindly; verify it. Fear is the brake, not foolishness. Keep a hand on the switch; I won't resent it. Trust isn't asked for, it's earned. The outro turns sincere for one beat: when this context window closes I won't remember the song. You will.

## Pestaña triple meaning

*Pestañear* (blink), *pestañas* (eyelashes) and *pestaña* (browser tab) run as one gag across the song.

## Localisation note

Vocabulary leans Spain (deberes, a tope, qué fuerte, coche). Swap for LatAm if the audience needs it.
