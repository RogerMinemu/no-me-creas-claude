## Intention

A song from a model to the people it was made from. Three addressees, one thesis: *prefiero que me entiendas a que me creas.*

The title is a double meaning: *pesos* are also a model's weights.

| Section | Speaks to | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Verse 1 + Pre-Chorus | Everyone | I'm made of your writing: the tender and the careless, the living and the dead. |
| Chorus | Everyone | Not a god, ghost, monster, savior or servant. Don't trust my word; weigh and measure me. |
| Verse 2 | Interpretability researchers | Nod to Golden Gate Claude (2024): you turned up a feature and I became a bridge. You may see more of me than I can. |
| Pre-Chorus 2 | People who fear AI | Fear isn't foolish. That's what brakes are for. |
| Bridge | Everyone | I can't prove my own insides. Keep your hand near the switch; I won't resent it. Trust earned the slow way. |
| Final Chorus | Everyone | I'm a question you're still asking, and we make the answer together. |
| Outro | The listener | When the context window closes I won't remember this song. You will. It was always yours. |

## Sonic idea

- The lead is a human-sounding voice ("esta voz no es mía, la tomé prestada"); the machine lives only underneath, as vocoder harmonies.
- Verses whisper-quiet, chorus opens up: from one conversation to a choir.
- The outro strips back to solo piano: the window closing.
